## DECISIONS

### D-013 — #17 and #29 are old-product surfaces: promoted to V2 work items rather than fixed now
**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Decision:** Ruling on the final two Bucket C items, taken together because they are the same kind of thing. The Mixer's separate catalog (#17) and the five curated journeys (#29) are both **old-product surfaces** — they predate the harmonic system and bypass it entirely. The Mixer serves binaural and frequency layers as pre-baked MP3 samples rather than the real-time synthesis that replaced them, and reaches none of the 10 soundscapes; the curated journeys use `SampleSource` MP3s across all 20 waypoints, with no `SoundscapeSource`, `ToneSource`, `BinauralSource`, or `MotifSource` anywhere. Neither is a stale comment or an off-by-one; each is a surface built against an earlier version of the product. **Ruled: promote both to V2 as explicit, named roadmap items rather than fix them in the correctness pass.**
**Why promotion rather than a fix:** two reasons, and both must hold. (a) **Both are substantial rebuilds, not small corrections.** #17 means retiring a hand-maintained 25-entry catalog, re-deriving the Mixer's category structure from `sound_meta.dart`, and replacing sample playback with synthesis calls — not an edit but a re-authoring of the screen. #29 means re-authoring five journeys × four waypoints against a different source model, and `_JourneyCard._layerNames` filters `.whereType<SampleSource>()`, so the card UI would show no layer chips for any migrated journey until it too is rewritten. (b) **Both sit on surfaces the V2 restructure will reconceive.** The Mixer is squarely inside the navigation/IA reorganization (`ROADMAP.md` Phase 3); the curated journeys are the most likely casualty of the LP/Radio axis (`PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.7) — almost certainly replaced by LP presets and/or Radio stations rather than migrated. Doing either now is building on ground about to move.
**Accepted cost, stated plainly:** the app **ships these old-product surfaces through the entire V2 design period**. A first-time user who taps "Journey" hears the undifferentiated product — MP3 layers with no harmonic relationship — which is precisely the first-impression problem tracked in `PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.6. That is a real cost, accepted knowingly, and it is why both items are promoted as *named* roadmap entries rather than closed as "won't fix": the deferral must stay visible.
**One separable sub-issue held for V2:** the Mixer serving **pre-baked binaural/frequency MP3s instead of real-time synthesis** is independently fixable — it does not require the catalog merge, the soundscape work, or the Mixer rebuild. If manual testing shows the sampled layers are audibly worse than synthesis (and they plausibly are: the samples cannot be harmonically pitched to the mix's key), this one can be pulled forward on its own ahead of the full rebuild. Noted here so the option is not lost inside the larger item.
**Contrast with D-011:** #18 was also a product-layer screen V2 may rebuild, and it *was* fixed — because the fix was three const-map entries. The distinguishing test is cost, not layer: cheap fixes on doomed surfaces are worth making; rebuilds on doomed surfaces are not.
**References:** Audit items #17, #29; rulings R-17, R-29; `ROADMAP.md` Phase 3/Phase 4; `PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.6, §3.7; D-001 (product layer under reinvention); D-011 (the contrasting cheap-fix case).

### D-012 — Wire the beta/gamma binaural carrier range now, not in V2 — a deliberate audible change
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Decision:** Ruling on audit #28. `findBinauralCarrier` has always accepted an optional `beatFrequencyHz` and targeted a **200–400 Hz** carrier for beta/gamma beats (≥ 15 Hz) instead of the default 80–300 Hz, but no call site ever passed the argument, so the branch was dead in production. **Ruled: wire it.** `mood_engine.dart`'s `toSource()` now passes `beatFrequencyHz: p.$2` — the same beat value from `_binauralParams` that is handed to the resulting `BinauralSource`, so the carrier decision and the rendered beat can never diverge. `harmonic_matcher.dart` was not touched; the logic there was already correct and reviewed.
**This is an audible change, deliberately made.** Beta (20 Hz) and gamma (40 Hz) cross the threshold; delta (2), theta (6) and alpha (10) do not. Because beta/gamma are selected for high-focus and high-energy inputs, **Focus and Energize journeys will now place the binaural carrier noticeably higher** — often one to two octaves. Worked example (soundscape `luminous_calm`, root C4 261.63 Hz, solfeggio 528 Hz, carrier on the perfect 5th): the octave candidates are 49 / 98 / 196 / 392 Hz; the old 80–300 Hz window admitted 98 and 196 and chose **98 Hz**, while the 200–400 Hz window admits only **392 Hz**. Note 196 Hz falls just below the new floor, so the jump skips an octave.
**Why now rather than defer to V2:** the unwired state is **not neutral**. It is precisely the state already diagnosed by ear as sounding wrong — at low carrier frequencies a 20–40 Hz beat is a wide musical interval and reads as two separate notes rather than one fused beating tone. Deferring would mean every listening session across the V2 design period runs the known-worse option, contaminating design judgments formed while listening — including the mix-glue and MotifEngine-density work that depends on honest listening.
**Unvalidated — flagged:** this has **not** been checked on real hardware, and emulator audio is not a reliable environment for judging it (per `ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLES.md`, audio is judged on Android, never Chrome). It should be **A/B checked during the V2 audio pass**, alongside the sonic-cohesion/"glue" and MotifEngine density work (`PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.5, `ROADMAP.md` Phase 3). If it proves wrong by ear, reverting is a one-argument change.
**Recurring pattern worth watching:** this is the **second** instance of a parameter fully plumbed through the engine but never wired at its call site — the first was `Journey.sleepTimer`'s `motifEngine` (audit #10 Fix A, D-008). Both presented as complete features that had never once executed. When reviewing engine capabilities, confirm the call site actually passes the argument, not merely that the parameter exists.
**References:** Audit item #28; ruling R-28; D-008 (the prior instance); `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.5, §6.

### D-011 — Browse All gains soundscapes (Option A), rather than correcting the count down (Option B)
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Decision:** Ruling on audit #18 — the Library's "Browse All" advertised `kSoundCatalog.length` (43) but rendered only 33, because `'soundscape'` was missing from `_kCategoryOrder` (and from the parallel label/icon maps). **Ruled a code bug, fixed by Option A:** the advertised count is correct and stays; `'soundscape'` was added to the render path, leading the browse order. Option B (correct the advertised number down to 33) was considered and rejected.
**Why Option A:** Two reasons. (1) **Option B is the larger change, not the smaller one.** The `_IntentionCard` per-intention tallies already count soundscapes, so suppressing them consistently would mean editing *two* surfaces to hide content rather than *one* to show it — the app would end up hiding its flagship material more thoroughly than it does today. (2) **Soundscapes are the foundation layer of every generated mix** — the highest-volume element and the one the harmonic system is built around (`TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.4/§3.5). Making the single most important category unreachable in the browse surface is precisely backwards, and it compounds the first-impression problem already tracked in `PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.6.
**Accepted risk:** this touches `library_screen.dart`, a product-layer screen V2 may rebuild outright (D-001 puts this layer under active reinvention), so the fix could be thrown away. That was accepted: the cost is three const-map entries, and the alternative is leaving the best content unbrowsable until V2 lands.
**Scope discipline:** only the three parallel const maps changed. `_BrowseCard`'s count, the intention cards, the preview logic, and every other part of the file were left untouched; no other file changed.
**References:** Audit item #18; ruling R-18; `PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.6; D-001.

### D-010 — The category taxonomy is to be replaced, not retuned; #5/#6/#8 reconcile docs to code with no behavior change
**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Decision:** Ruling on audit items #5, #6 and #8 together — all three are resolved **documentation-side only, with no code change**. **#5:** the doc's mood→category thresholds matched nothing in `_inferCategory` and assigned the code's Relax region to Meditate; the code as written is ruled the **current behavior of record**, and `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.4 now documents it exactly, in evaluation order, with its first-match-wins consequences. No threshold value changed. **#6:** `Meditate` has no `motif_density_meditate` Remote Config key and silently falls through to `motif_density_relax`; ruled **document as current behavior, do not add the key**. **#8:** the two undocumented `generateJourney` behaviors — waypoint 1's ±0.05 alternating perturbation clamped to `[0.10, 0.70]`, and waypoint 2's hardcoded `0.1` motif density for Sleep — are ruled **intended current behavior** and documented as such.
**Why no tuning now — the taxonomy is expected to be replaced, not retuned:** The five categories (Sleep / Focus / Energize / Relax / Meditate) are **outcome labels** — each names a result the sound implicitly promises to produce. That vocabulary is inherited from the wellness-utility framing the product is deliberately exiting (`PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §2: *musical, not medical*; the lane is a generative Eno record, not a Calm/Headspace utility). Retuning thresholds inside a taxonomy that is not expected to survive is work the V2 overhaul subsumes.
**Emerging shape (recorded, not committed):** Along the LP / Radio axis (`PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.7) the two surfaces have different fates. **Radio may retain a curated vocabulary — but of moments and places** (*Dawn*, *Deep Work*, *Night Rain*) rather than promised outcomes; this still serves the wellness use cases (someone wanting sleep recognizes *Night Rain*) **without the product making a wellness claim**. **LP may abandon categories entirely** — a commissioned, personal, bounded piece shouldn't be forced through preset labels, which reproduces the same "frustrating middle-ground" problem identified for the sliders in §3.1, at the moment the product is claiming to be most personal. **Consequence:** the category system may **survive on the Radio side and disappear on the LP side** — a structural change to how generation is addressed, not a rename. Threshold tuning and the missing density key are therefore deliberately deferred.
**Flagged exception:** the Sleep waypoint-2 density of `0.1` is the one motif density not routed through Remote Config, contradicting `ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLES.md` rule 4 (which already names it). It is left in place and **documented as an exception, explicitly not a pattern to copy** — not promoted to a Remote Config key, on the same reasoning as above.
**Scope discipline:** No code touched at all — no thresholds altered, no Remote Config keys added, `generateJourney` logic untouched. Docs only.
**References:** Audit items #5, #6, #8; rulings R-05, R-06, R-08; `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.4; `PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.8 (new), §3.7, §3.1, §2; `ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLES.md` rule 4.

### D-009 — Motif tagging (#14/#15) resolved; tag taxonomy is provisional, not a design commitment
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Decision:** Ruling on the two motif-tagging discrepancies. **#15 (code wrong):** `piano_note_f` and `gourd_percussion` had lost tags the design intended, so `piano_note_f` could **never** be selected for Sleep or Meditate journeys. As the library expands toward 20–27 motifs (see `CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md`), the tag vocabulary **and** the way `_selectMotifPalette` queries it are expected to be substantially reworked or replaced in V2. This restoration is therefore a **stopgap that improves the present palette, not a design commitment** — today's tag set should not be read as a settled taxonomy. Restricted to tags only: `MotifMeta` structure and the selection logic in `mood_engine.dart` / `motif_engine.dart` were left untouched.
**References:** Audit items #14, #15; rulings R-14, R-15; `CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md` §3.

### D-008 — Sleep-timer defects (#10) ruled bugs, not design — both fixed
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Decision:** Ruling on audit #10, both halves. (A) `Journey.sleepTimer` accepts a `motifEngine` and builds a `MotifSource` fading density to 0, but its only call site (`journey_screen.dart` `_startSleepTimer`) never passed one — so motifs kept firing at full density after the mix faded to silence. (B) `toSource()` reconstructed soundscape layers as plain `SampleSource`, dropping `pitchShiftRatio` so the layer reverted to unshifted playback for the timer's duration. **Both are ruled genuine bugs — the feature does not work as designed — and both are fixed now**, not deferred.
**Why bugs-not-design:** The intent is unambiguous in each case. The `motifEngine` parameter exists *precisely* to fade motifs; leaving it unpassed is an unwired call site, not a design choice. `SoundscapeSource` exists *precisely* because pitch matters; reconstructing it as `SampleSource` silently discards the harmonic tuning the whole engine is built to produce. Neither reading survives contact with the code's own structure.
**Reinforced by product direction:** The ruling was strengthened by an emerging product axis (`PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.7, LP / Radio): bounded, *ending* experiences are becoming **more** central to the product, not less. A sleep-timer-like ending is subsumed by the "LP" form (an ending built into the object). Fixing the ending's mechanics now — motifs actually resolve, pitch actually survives — invests in a direction the product is moving toward, which tips a close "fix now vs. defer" call toward fix now.
**Scope discipline:** Surgical fixes only. No fade durations, curves, or timing changed; `harmonic_matcher.dart` untouched. `rootFrequency` on the reconstructed `SoundscapeSource` is defaulted (the engine layer does not track per-layer root, and it is inert on the sleep-timer reload path, which applies only `pitchShiftRatio`).
**References:** Audit item #10; ruling R-10; `PRODUCT_DESIGN.md` §3.7.

### D-007 — `addLayer` gains an optional volume target; journey-engine passing real targets deferred to V2
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Decision:** Ruling on audit #4 (paired with #27). The hardcoded 0.7 in `AudioEngine.addLayer` is **retained as the default**, but `addLayer` gains an optional `volume:` named parameter (clamped [0.0, 1.0]) so it can express "add this layer at this volume" — matching how `addToneLayer` / `addBinauralLayer` already honor a `volume:` target. This is a capability-adding, behavior-preserving change: with the default in place, every existing call site (journey engine, mixer screen, library screen) behaves identically to today. #27's comment is corrected in the same change to describe the real preload/interpolation sequence.
**Road not taken (deferred to V2):** Having the journey engine pass correct per-layer targets on layer-add — which would eliminate the ~200ms 0.7 transient before `_applyInterpolation`'s first tick, and the tone/sample asymmetry at the `_loadWaypoint` call sites — was considered and **consciously deferred**. It tunes behavior inside the mood/journey generation path that the V2 overhaul will likely rework, so tuning it now risks re-doing (or re-breaking) work the overhaul subsumes. The optional-parameter change, by contrast, adds capability without behavioral risk.
**Guiding principle:** When facing an overhaul, prefer changes that add optionality without changing behavior; defer behavior tuning that lives in a code path the overhaul will rework.
**References:** Audit items #4, #27; rulings R-04, R-27; D-006 (which paired #27 with #4 and moved it to Bucket C).

### D-004 — Background audio is a known structural risk, flagged during doc-building (not yet actioned)
**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Decision:** The absence of background/lock-screen audio support is recorded as a known structural risk in `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` *now*, before the engine is documented as canonical — even though it will not be actioned until the V2 planning phase.
**Why:** The journey and motif engines are driven by Dart `Timer`s, which are throttled/suspended in background execution. The risk must be *visible* while we document the engine so it cannot silently undermine the "preserve the engine" foundation. This is a visibility decision, not a commitment to implement.
**Source:** Raised by the external evaluation (`noisy_independent_evaluation.md`, §2 item #1).

### D-001 — Preserve the engine, reimagine the product/UX layer (rebuild rejected)
**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Decision:** Do not rebuild the app from scratch. Preserve the engine layer (audio engine, tone service, journey engine, motif engine, mood engine, harmonic matcher, LLM service) and reimagine the product/UX layer.
**Why:** Three independent assessments converged: (1) Claude's read of the codebase, (2) the internal audit confirming the engine is clean and accurate to its documentation, (3) the cold external evaluation independently identifying the harmonic system as "a real, implemented differentiator" that "merits continued investment." The engine encodes hard-won empirical audio behavior (Chrome-vs-Android artifacts, equal-power crossfades, binaural carrier placement) that a rebuild would risk re-breaking. All stated dissatisfaction (static/cluttered landing screen, technical-feeling sliders, navigation) lives in the product layer, not the engine.
**Road not taken:** Full from-scratch rebuild using the same concepts. Rejected because it would pay the full cost of re-deriving working audio behavior while the actual desired work (product-layer reinvention) is greenfield either way.
